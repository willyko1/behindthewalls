Before you book a viewing
  1. Compare the actual apartment’s rent, size, utilities, and fees with the area context.
  2. Open its address profile below and look for repeat reporting across months.
  3. Ask management about repairs, heat, hot water, and how maintenance requests are handled.
  4. Check official building records and plan your commute before signing.

Rental context comes from StreetEasy’s public monthly market download, retrieved October 28, 2026, with latest observations for September 2026. The complaint snapshot covers October 2025–May 2026. These are different periods and geographic definitions. Neighborhood rent matches use names; unmatched areas receive an explicitly labeled borough benchmark. Missing rental values are never treated as zero or carried forward.
